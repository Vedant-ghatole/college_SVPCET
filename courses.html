Courses Offered

St. Vincent Pallotti College of Engineering and Technology (SVPCET) in Nagpur offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and technology.

Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech):

Civil Engineering

Focuses on infrastructure development, construction technologies, and environmental sustainability.

Computer Engineering

Deals with software development, hardware integration, and emerging technologies like AI and ML.

Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering

Specializes in communication systems, embedded systems, and networking technologies.

Electrical Engineering

Covers power systems, electrical machines, and control systems.

Information Technology

Focuses on software engineering, cybersecurity, and data analytics.

Mechanical Engineering

Includes robotics, thermal engineering, and manufacturing technologies.

Artificial Intelligence

Concentrates on machine learning, neural networks, and deep learning applications.

Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science)

Focuses on big data analytics, machine learning, and cloud computing.

Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security)

Deals with ethical hacking, network security, and cyber threat intelligence.

Industrial Internet of Things (IoT)

Emphasizes IoT applications in industries, smart devices, and automation.

Computer Science and Business Systems (in collaboration with TCS)

Integrates business processes with computing solutions.

Postgraduate Programs (M.Tech):

M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering

Advanced topics in software development, AI, and computational intelligence.

M.Tech. in CAD/CAM (Mechanical Engineering)

Specializes in computer-aided design and manufacturing techniques.

Vocational Programs (B.Voc):

B.Voc. in Software Development

Practical exposure to full-stack development and software engineering.

B.Voc. in Cyber Security

Hands-on training in ethical hacking and digital forensics.

B.Voc. in Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality

Explores immersive technologies, gaming, and real-world applications.
